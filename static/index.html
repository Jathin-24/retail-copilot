        Architectural Foundations
        
      
        
        Strict separation of concerns ensures absolute numerical accuracy and zero hallucinations:
      
        
      
        
        
        
          
        1. Deterministic Python Core
        
          
        
            All aggregations, run-rates, margins, safety stock calculations, and anomaly detections are executed deterministically in Python using SQLite and Pandas.
          
        
        
        
        
        
          
        2. Grounded Gemini Reasoning
        
          
        
            Gemini performs intent classification and explains calculated outputs. Gemini never calculates or invents numerical figures.
          
        
        
        
      
        
    
        

    
        
      
        Module Boundaries
        
      
        
        The codebase is partitioned into isolated, modular domains:
      
        
      
        
        
        
          
        
            database/
            
        Stores & Inventory DB
        
            
        Local SQLite persistence with stores, products, inventory, and sales.
        
          
        
        
        
        
        
          
        
            analytics/
            
        Deterministic KPIs
        
            
        Sales velocity, product performance, and gross profit metrics.
        
          
        
        
        
        
        
          
        
            forecasting/
            
        Demand Run-Rate
        
            
        Days of supply projection and stockout risk runway modeling.
        
          
        
        
        
        
        
          
        
            anomaly/
            
        Anomaly Detection
        
            
        Statistical detection of sales spikes, sudden drops, and overstocks.
        
          
        
        
        
        
        
          
        
            recommendations/
            
        Action Engine
        
            
        Automated reordering, inter-store transfers, and priority actions.
        
          
        
        
        
        
        
          
        
            copilot/
            
        Gemini Reasoning
        
            
        Natural-language intent classification and grounded explanations.
        
          
        
        
        
        
        
          
        
            retrieval/
            
        Local Search
        
            
        Fast catalog and supplier metadata querying without external APIs.
